Amplifier Channel Load Calculator

Constant-voltage (70V / 100V) systems are additive — total channel wattage is simply the tap setting times the number of speakers on that tap. This calculates required amplifier channel wattage from a single tap group, plus recommended headroom sizing.

Constant-voltage (70V/100V) taps are power-additive regardless of line voltage — impedance is not part of the load calculation. Recommended amp rating adds 20% headroom, per standard industry practice, to avoid running the channel at full rated output.
